# Privacy Policy

**Chat Image Rotate** (the "extension") does not collect, store, transmit,
sell, or share any personal data.

## What the extension does
It runs only on `https://web.whatsapp.com/`. It looks at the image element
shown in WhatsApp Web's image viewer and applies a CSS rotation to it on your
device. This happens entirely inside your browser.

## What it does not do
- It makes no network requests
- It does not read, copy, or send your messages, contacts, or media
- It does not use cookies, local storage, or analytics
- It does not include any third-party code, trackers, or ads

## Permissions
The extension requests no special browser permissions. It runs a content script
on web.whatsapp.com only, so it can add the rotate button and shortcut.
